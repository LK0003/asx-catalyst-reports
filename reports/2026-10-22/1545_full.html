What changed vs this morning: At 9:35am this was "terms not yet verified." Now largely confirmed, with only the exact NTA/capital-gain figures single-sourced.

Financial impact: Material — ~39% distribution guidance increase and ~19% FFO increase for an A-REIT of this size, even accounting for the special distribution being a one-off.

Expectation/surprise: Partially anticipated (halt flagged "a material transaction"), but the magnitude and the full pivot out of direct property are a genuine positive surprise.

Market/sector context: A-REIT sector; capital recycling into lending has been a 2026 theme — GDF's full pivot is a sharper version of it.

Technical/price-reaction: Confirmed strong reaction — delayed quote (~2:09pm AEDT) showed GDF at A$1.1200, up +9.27% vs prior close A$1.0250.

Key risks:

  • The 10cps special distribution is a one-off capital return tied to CGT treatment, not sustainable earnings.
  • Sale price vs. book/NTA and the $90.7m capital-gain figure remain single-sourced.
  • Garda is now a smaller, lending-focused entity — a structural risk-profile change some holders may not have signed up for.

What to watch: Whether the +9.27% move holds into the close; broker re-rating notes; confirmation of exact NTA/capital-gain figures from the primary PDF.
