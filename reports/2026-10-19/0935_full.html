What to watch: The specific new guidance number and stated rationale; read-through to other childcare-exposed landlords.

Section 8 — Best 3 to Research Further

1. CGF (Challenger)

Why it made the list: Largest, most unambiguous price-sensitive event of the morning — a specific, priced, named-bidder takeover approach for a major ASX financial institution.

What the market may be reacting to: A ~18–20% premium full-takeover proposal from an already-substantial shareholder, validating value while raising completion-probability questions.

What would confirm the thesis: A board recommendation, an improved/firmed-up offer, early signs of regulatory comfort (APRA/FIRB).

What would invalidate it: Board rejection as inadequate, a lapsed/withdrawn proposal (as happened with Challenger's own Pepper Money approach earlier in 2026), or a material regulatory objection.

Biggest risk: Completion risk on a non-binding, conditional proposal.

What to monitor: Primary ASX text and price terms; board statements; analyst target revisions; any competing approach.

2. CWY (Cleanaway)

Why it made the list: Moves from non-binding proposal to a signed, binding Scheme Implementation Deed — the clearest "deal de-risking" event in this report.

What the market may be reacting to: Confirmation that months of exclusivity/due-diligence extensions concluded in an actual contract, not a further delay or walk-away.

What would confirm the thesis: Favourable independent expert opinion; FIRB/ACCC clearances progressing unconditionally; scheme meeting date set.

What would invalidate it: A disclosed price cut or adverse term change vs. $3.13; ACCC concerns given Cleanaway's scale; a superior proposal before the vote.

Biggest risk: Regulatory (ACCC) timeline/conditions risk.

What to monitor: Primary SID text; ACCC's public register; scheme meeting and court dates.

3. ARF (Arena REIT)

Why it made the list: A guidance upgrade against an explicitly conservative, worst-case prior floor tied to a well-known tenant-default overhang — a de-risking story with a clear fundamental driver.

What the market may be reacting to: Likely progress on resolving the Edge Early Learning administration (e.g. a sale of the Edge portfolio to a going-concern operator such as Goodstart).

What would confirm the thesis: The new guidance figure sitting meaningfully above the 18.0cps floor, attributed to resolved/improved Edge income.

What would invalidate it: A disappointingly small upgrade, or one driven by something unrelated to Edge.

Biggest risk: Residual Edge uncertainty — even a resolved sale can involve rent abatement or a lower-paying replacement tenant.

What to monitor: The primary announcement's specific guidance figure and rationale; read-through to other childcare-exposed REITs.
