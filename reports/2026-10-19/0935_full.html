1. CGF — Challenger Limited — Takeover approach from TAL Dai-ichi Life

8:01am AEDTTakeoverPrice-sensitive: Yes🟢 DirectionScore: 82/100

Why it matters: TAL Dai-ichi Life Australia (TDA) — the Daiichi Life Insurance unit that built a ~20.5% stake in Challenger through 2025 purchases from MS&AD and Apollo — has lodged a non-binding, conditional proposal to acquire the remaining ~79.5% it doesn't already own, via scheme of arrangement, at A$11.80/share (corroborated independently as ~17.7% premium to the 1-month VWAP of $10.03). This converts a long-running strategic-shareholder relationship into a credible full-takeover approach — a genuine, not-previously-priced-in surprise.

What changed: ~20.5% strategic shareholder → conditional 100% scheme proposal.

Financial impact: Implies ~A$7.9bn equity value. Same-morning Q1 FY27 update showed AUM +5% YoY to $31.9bn and total sales +52% YoY to $4.4bn — a strong standalone quarter.

Expectation/surprise: Genuine surprise; board says shareholders need take no action yet. Not verified against any analyst consensus target.

Key risks:

  • Indicative and non-binding — due diligence, a Scheme Implementation Deed, and board recommendation are all still outstanding.
  • Board has only said it is "considering" the proposal.
  • Regulatory approvals could add delay or conditions.
  • A rival bidder, or TDA walking away after due diligence, would reverse today's move.

What to watch: Opening price gap vs. $11.80 indicative price; ASX price query response; board's formal response statement.

Sources: Money Management · Super Review · Capital Brief · Investing.com/Reuters
