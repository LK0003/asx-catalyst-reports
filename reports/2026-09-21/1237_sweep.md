- **Time:** Investor Presentation lodged with ASX "today" per search snippets (exact time not verified — feed blocked).

EGM planned November 2026; close targeted by end FY26, subject to Telix shareholder vote, regulatory approvals and customary conditions.
- **Price-sensitive:** Y.
- **Price reaction:** Reported down ~3–6% intraday (one source: A$17.31, -3.03%; another headline cites >6% initial fall) — **not independently verified**, network blocked.
- **Materiality take:** This is a case where the headline ("merger," "radiopharmaceutical powerhouse," combined 2026 revenue >US$1.3bn) reads positive but the market's actual reaction is negative — the deal implies ~31% dilution to Telix's share count, funded almost entirely in scrip, which is the more likely driver of the share-price fall than any operational read-through. Strategically it locks in global Lu-177 supply (relevant after ITM's own recent FDA setback, per background reporting), but near-term the dilution/scrip-heavy structure appears to be dominating sentiment. Flagging this as a real gap: this should have been the top item in the AM full report and was missed (both AM reports cited only the stale Pixclara FDA approval news for TLX).

### 2. INA — Ingenia Communities Group

- **Headline:** Ingenia's board has rejected a revised, non-binding indicative proposal from Warburg Pincus of $5.05 cash per stapled security (up from a prior $4.75 proposal, which was itself a revision of an earlier ~$1.4bn/A$1.9bn approach) — board says it undervalues the company and is not in securityholders' best interests. - **Price-sensitive:** Y.
- **Price reaction:** +2.31% to A$4.42 at 11:35 AEST — still ~14.3% below the rejected $5.05 offer price, suggesting the market is not fully backing the board's stand-alone case. **Not independently re-verified beyond this snippet.**

The "still 14% below offer" gap is the more informative data point than the rejection itself — it signals the market sees meaningful takeover-premium risk if this is the final round, similar in shape to today's PPT/EQT situation. Not new information about Ingenia's underlying business, only a capital-structure/M&A-probability update.
