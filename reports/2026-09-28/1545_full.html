Expectation/surprise: A genuine negative surprise — an unplanned equipment failure, not a previously flagged risk, and material relative to prior guidance (roughly a 10–14% reduction at the low end for Baúna specifically).

Market/sector context: Compounds an already-soft session for energy majors (Section 6) and follows prior Baúna operational issues reported earlier in the year.
