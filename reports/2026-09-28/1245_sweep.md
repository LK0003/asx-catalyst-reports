### 1. NST — Northern Star Resources rejects Gold Fields' ~A$38.7bn takeover approach — **Price-sensitive: YES** — missed by both earlier reports today
- **What:** Northern Star's board unanimously rejected a non-binding, conditional proposal from Gold Fields (received 14 September 2026: 0.3125 new Gold Fields shares + A$7.25 cash per NST share, initially valued ~A$38.7bn / a 22% premium to the 11 Sept close, but down to ~A$36.1bn on Gold Fields' Friday close). - **Timing:** Confirmed in an ASX release "this morning" (28 Sept); market reaction was immediate — NST shares reportedly jumped as much as ~11% at the open to ~A$24.46 before settling ~8.3% higher (~A$23.95). This should have been caught by the 09:36 full report or 10:16 sweep but was not — both runs only had generic gold-price read-through for NST/CMM with no company-specific news identified, evidently due to the access restriction above. Flagging now as newly confirmed.
- **Materiality take:** Clearly material — a rejected ~$38bn all-scrip-plus-cash approach for the ASX's largest pure-play gold producer is a genuine catalyst, not noise. Key open question for follow-through: whether Gold Fields returns with an improved/binding offer (per the board's objection to price, not principle) or walks away — watch for further ASX releases from either party. Note reported deal-value figures vary across outlets (A$38.7bn, A$36.1bn, and some describe it in different currency/lower terms) — treat the ~$27-38bn range as unverified pending the primary ASX document.
