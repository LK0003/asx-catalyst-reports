### 2. TCG — Turaco Gold — "Turaco to Consolidate Afema Ownership" (10:29am)
- **Catalyst type:** Acquisition. **Price-sensitive:** Yes. **Catalyst Score: 50/100.** **Direction: 🟡**
- **Why it matters:** Turaco has executed agreements to acquire Sodim Ltd's remaining **20% interest** in the 4.65Moz Afema Gold Project (Côte d'Ivoire), taking Turaco to **100% ownership** (subject to the State's 10% non-contributing carried interest). Reported deal value ~US$79m/A$112m, structured as US$30m cash, ~55.1m Turaco shares, ~36.7m unlisted options and a 0.33% net smelter royalty to Sodim. This resolves the remaining minority overhang at Turaco's flagship asset.
- **What changed:** Ownership path from 51% (Nov 2023) → 80% (Dec 2024) → now agreed 100%, removing Sodim as a minority partner and eliminating the ~US$24m Sodim loan and up to US$2.5m discovery payment/1.5% NSR conversion right that came with the prior structure.
- **Financial impact:** Simplifies the cap structure and future cash-flow entitlement at Afema (no more minority carry to service), at the cost of near-term dilution (new shares/options to Sodim) plus a cash component that reportedly requires a fresh equity placement and Share Purchase Plan to fund.
- **Expectation/surprise:** Moderate positive — full consolidation has been a logical next step given the 2023→2024 trajectory, but the timing (agreed, not yet shareholder-approved) and the need for fresh equity to fund the cash leg are a real dilution consideration.
- **Market/sector context:** Gold sector has been well-bid in 2026 (spot ~US$4,000–4,027/oz); a 100%-owned, single large asset is a cleaner equity story for a gold developer in this environment.
- **Technical/price context:** Not verified — TCG has been in a trading halt since 9:35am this morning and, per the ASX announcements page, remains halted as of this scan; no reinstatement notice found. This Afema announcement does not appear to be the halt-ending release.
- **Key risks:** Deal is **agreed, not completed** — subject to shareholder approval expected early December 2026; funding for the cash component is not yet in place; TCG's underlying halt cause (from 9:35am) is still separately unresolved.
- **What to watch:** The actual halt-ending announcement (separate from this Afema news); terms and pricing of the flagged equity placement/SPP; December shareholder vote.
- **Sources:** [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/asx-stock-news/mining/tcg-turaco-gold-afema-acquisition-october-2026/); [ASX TCG announcements](https://www.asx.com.au/asx/v2/statistics/announcements.do?by=asxCode&asxCode=TCG); background via [Ecofin Agency](https://www.ecofinagency.com/mining/1712-46259-cote-divoire-turaco-gold-now-holds-80-of-afema-gold-project).
