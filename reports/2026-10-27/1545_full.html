Financial impact: Materially de-risks the ~A$520m total Donald Project funding plan (50:50 target debt/equity gearing); Astron and JV partner Energy Fuels (targeting 49% via A$183m of upfront equity funding) both filed simultaneously, confirming the project-level nature of the news.

Expectation/surprise: Positive but incremental — the market has been tracking this financing process since late 2025; today's approval is the next expected step, not a bolt-from-the-blue catalyst, and it is explicitly conditional, not a binding loan.

Market/sector context: Government co-financing of a non-China rare earths project is a sector-supportive signal beyond just ATR/EF2 (see Section 6).

Technical/price context: Not verified — could not retrieve a live intraday quote for ATR or EF2 this scan.

Type: Acquisition Price-sensitive: Yes Score: 50/100 🟡 Direction

Why it matters: Turaco has executed agreements to acquire Sodim Ltd's remaining 20% interest in the 4.65Moz Afema Gold Project (Côte d'Ivoire), taking Turaco to 100% ownership (subject to the State's 10% non-contributing carried interest). Reported deal value ~US$79m/A$112m, structured as US$30m cash, ~55.1m Turaco shares, ~36.7m unlisted options and a 0.33% net smelter royalty to Sodim. This resolves the remaining minority overhang at Turaco's flagship asset.

Financial impact: Simplifies the cap structure and future cash-flow entitlement at Afema, at the cost of near-term dilution (new shares/options to Sodim) plus a cash component reportedly requiring a fresh equity placement and SPP.

Expectation/surprise: Moderate positive — full consolidation was a logical next step, but the timing (agreed, not yet shareholder-approved) and the need for fresh equity are a real dilution consideration.

Market/sector context: Gold well-bid in 2026 (spot ~US$4,000–4,027/oz); a 100%-owned single large asset is a cleaner equity story in this environment.

Technical/price context: Not verified — TCG has been in a trading halt since 9:35am and, per the ASX feed, remains halted as of this scan; no reinstatement notice found. This Afema announcement does not appear to be the halt-ending release.

Key risks:

  • Deal is agreed, not completed — subject to shareholder approval expected early December 2026.
  • Funding for the cash component is not yet in place.
  • TCG's underlying halt cause (from 9:35am) is still separately unresolved.

What to watch: The actual halt-ending announcement; terms/pricing of the flagged equity placement/SPP; December shareholder vote.
