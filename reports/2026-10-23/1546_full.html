1. ZIP — Zip Co 🔴 Score 88/100

Time: Announcement lodged 8:22am (flagged this morning as "content not verified"); this entry reflects the actual result and confirmed market reaction · Type: Earnings/Guidance — trading update · Price-sensitive: Yes

Why it matters: The single biggest market-moving event of the day. Zip disclosed a coordinated external fraud event in the US (individuals opening new accounts with legitimate credentials and running up unpaid transactions), costing US$7.2m (A$10.1m) in one-off cash operating expenses and temporarily denting US TTV/new-customer growth in July–August (recovering in September, +41% USD y/y). Net bad debts rose to 1.97% of TTV (vs 1.65% in 1Q FY26) and active customers were flat y/y / down 1.7% q/q. The market reaction — shares down ~30% intraday to ~A$1.45 — shows credit-quality/fraud-control concerns outweighing the headline growth numbers.

What changed: Actual 1Q FY27 print replaces this morning's placeholder: TTV +18% y/y to A$4.6bn (US TTV +34.1%), total income +17% y/y to ~A$373–376m (sources differ slightly, not reconciled), cash EBTDA +3.4% to A$65m (would have been A$75.1m, +19.5%, excluding the fraud cost), cash NTM 3.8% (down from 4.0%). FY27 guidance (group cash EBTDA A$340m, US TTV growth >30% USD, 20–22% operating margin) was explicitly reaffirmed.

Financial impact: High and two-sided. RBC Capital Markets' Julian Mulcahy kept an Outperform rating and A$2.07 target, saying the quarter "beat expectations on most key metrics" — yet the scale of the sell-off suggests the market is pricing a genuine trust/quality-of-earnings concern, not just this quarter's numbers.

Expectation/surprise: Very high — a textbook case of a metrics "beat" still reading as a negative surprise once a fraud/credit-quality flag is attached. The size of the price move vastly exceeds what the reported metrics alone would suggest.

Market/sector context: BNPL/consumer-fintech read-through turns negative (update from this morning's "neutral, pending the print" call) — watch for peer sympathy weakness into the close.

Technical/price-reaction: Confirmed ~30% intraday fall (Capital Brief: -29.9% to $1.45 at 12:21pm AEDT; Rask Media separately reports as much as ~50% intraday before a partial recovery) — one of the largest single-day moves in a liquid ASX200 stock seen in this report series.

Key risks:

  • Second fraud/credit-quality scare within roughly a year raises a structural question about risk controls scaling with US growth.
  • Active customers and merchants fell q/q (merchants down after exiting New Zealand) — a growth-quality flag independent of the fraud event.
  • RBC's Mulcahy explicitly warned a further sell-off "could occur today," by analogy to the FY26 Q2 loss-rate precedent — i.e. this may not be fully priced in.

What to watch: Whether the stock stabilises or extends losses into the 4pm close; additional broker notes on loss-rate trajectory; management commentary on fraud-control remediation; BNPL-peer price action.

Sources: Rask Media, Capital Brief, ASX feed 8:22am 23 Oct 2026.

2. EXR (Elixir Energy) / BPT (Beach Energy) 🟢 Score 72/100

Time: Binding agreements effective 22 Oct 2026; headlines lodged 9:26am this morning; terms confirmed via secondary sources this afternoon · Type: Partnership/Farmout · Price-sensitive: Yes (both sides)

Why it matters: This morning's report flagged the EXR/BPT headlines as the top "verify immediately" item, given a genuinely uncertain three-week standoff (exclusivity lapsed 1 October without a signed deal). That uncertainty is now resolved with real numbers: total transaction value A$205m, split as A$25m cash to Elixir plus a A$180m gross carry (Beach-funded appraisal drilling, seismic, G&G studies and operating costs for up to 4 years), of which Elixir's own notional share of the carried program is A$90m. Working interests move from Elixir's 50%/100%-operated pre-deal positions across ATP2056, ATP2057, ATP2044 and ATP2077-A/B down to 25%/50% (Beach taking operatorship of ATP2044 and ATP2077-A/B); Elixir retains its 49%-operated Diona-JV position untouched.

What changed: Confirms this is a binding agreement (not a non-binding heads of agreement), though completion is conditional, not yet done. No securities issued, no Board/management changes, no EXR shareholder approval required.

Financial impact: Materially de-risks Elixir's balance sheet — a fully carried, multi-year appraisal program across its core Taroom acreage without further dilutive equity raises, assuming completion proceeds. For Beach, a modest, strategic incremental east-coast gas position.

Expectation/surprise: Lower than this morning's framing, since the Beach-wins outcome is no longer new — the surprise value here is specifically the disclosed terms (A$205m, 50% program funding).

Market/sector context: Reinforces the SUPPORTED east-coast domestic gas thematic flagged this morning.

Key risks:

  • Completion is conditional on Ministerial Approval, ACCC approval/waiver, completion of Elixir's own farm-in to ATP2056, and a JV partner's pre-emption waiver — expected completion is 2H FY27, several months away.
  • Reported valuation metrics (~A$118,700/BCFe deal value vs ~A$39,888/BCFe implied by EXR's trading value) imply a rich valuation relative to the market price, if accurate — single secondary aggregator, not verified against primary disclosure.

What to watch: EXR/BPT price action; whether the retained-acreage valuation gap (if the cited numbers hold up) prompts broker re-rating notes.
