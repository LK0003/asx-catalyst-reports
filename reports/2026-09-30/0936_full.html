Why it matters
A federal court (Judge Christina Snyder, C.D. Cal.) issued a preliminary injunction on 10 Aug 2026 blocking operations at Dateline's Colosseum project in the Mojave National Preserve (NPCA v. Since then, both Dateline/Colosseum Rare Metals (18 Sep) and now the DOJ/Department of War (25 Sep, then again 28 Sep) have filed motions to stay that injunction pending appeal — the government's latest filing is backed by a sworn declaration from the Dept of War's Assistant Secretary for Industrial Base Policy citing national-security grounds. What changed

Financial impact
None yet — the project remains enjoined; any reversal would be a binary, high-impact positive for Dateline, but this is not yet decided.
Expectation/surprise
Government intervention on national-security grounds in a domestic mining injunction case is unusual and a moderate positive surprise for the stock's litigation risk profile, but the outcome remains genuinely uncertain pending the 26 Oct hearing.
Market/sector context
Fits the broader US critical-minerals/rare-earths supply-security push (see Section 6/non-ASX-native catalysts).
Technical context
Not verified.
Key risks

2. Dateline Resources (DTR)

Why it made the list
A rare instance of the US government itself filing court motions — backed by a national-security declaration — to unwind an injunction against an ASX-listed company's US project.
What the market may be reacting to
The shift from a purely adverse legal posture (August injunction) to active government support for the mine proceeding, changing the risk/reward skew ahead of the 26 October hearing.
What would confirm/invalidate the thesis
A stay of the injunction (or its denial) at or before the 26 October hearing; any further DOJ/Dept of War filings in the interim.
Biggest risk
Court timelines are unpredictable and a stay is not a ruling on the merits — the underlying environmental/preserve litigation could still ultimately go against the project.
What to monitor
PACER/court filings in NPCA v. US Government (C.D. Cal.), and any ASX announcement from Dateline referencing the case.
