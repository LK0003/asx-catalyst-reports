5. RIO / BHP — Resolution Copper / Oak Flat (non-ASX-native)

Time: 9th Circuit ruling, 29 Sep 2026 Type: Litigation / Regulatory approval Price-sensitive: Y (marginal) Score: 50/100 Direction: 🟢

Why it matters: The 9th US Circuit Court of Appeals denied en banc rehearing of its March 2026 panel decision clearing the Oak Flat land transfer to Resolution Copper — a Rio Tinto/BHP joint venture. The ruling carried an unusually sharp dissent: 10 of 11 dissenting judges wrote "We were wrong," calling it their "last chance to prevent the irreversible desecration" of the Apache sacred site; Chief Judge Murguia also dissented. The denial clears the way for the land exchange to proceed, subject to the underlying merits case continuing in District Court.

What changed

A procedural but consequential step removing (for now) one legal obstacle to a major future US copper project for RIO/BHP.

Financial impact

Immaterial to RIO/BHP's current earnings given their scale, but supports long-term optionality on a major copper development amid elevated copper prices.

Expectation / surprise

Broadly expected given the March panel decision, though the intensity of the dissent is notable and signals continued legal/political controversy ahead.

Sector / market context

Copper trading near record highs (~US$6.70–6.80/lb) — a supportive backdrop for any copper-development news.

Technical / price-reaction context

Both RIO and BHP were up ~1% today within a broad market rally — the move cannot be specifically attributed to this news.

Key risks
  • Underlying merits litigation continues in District Court
  • Further appeals (potentially to the Supreme Court) remain possible
  • Sacred-site controversy could yet see political intervention
What to watch

Any District Court scheduling or further appeal filings; possible Congressional or Executive-branch reaction given the dissent's prominence.
