What would confirm/invalidate the thesis: A strategic-review outcome that redesigns the trial with a credible path forward would partially invalidate the bearish read; a decision to wind down or materially de-prioritise the program would confirm it.

Why it made the list: A still-unresolved, binary situation — physical operations at the company's only producing asset remain suspended, with a hard 30 November deadline for the market to receive more information.

Biggest risk: Unknown scope of the underlying cyber incident — the company itself says it cannot yet assess the guidance impact.
