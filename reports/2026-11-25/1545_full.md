### 1. PTX — Prescient Therapeutics — Phase 2a dose-finding failure, −62.7%
**Time:** ~8:58am (not in the 9:39am report; first flagged by the 12:49pm sweep). **Type:** Clinical milestone (negative). **Price-sensitive:** Y. **Score: 68/100.** 🔴

**Why it matters:** The Dose Optimisation Committee reviewing Prescient's Phase 2a PTX-100 trial in cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (CTCL) reported only "modest efficacy," with 6 of 20 evaluable patients showing disease progression. The Committee found the data insufficient to select a dose, and recommended enrolment continue rather than advancing to a defined dose — effectively an inconclusive, unfavourable readout at a trial stage investors had expected to produce a clear go/no-go signal.

**What changed:** A dose-selection milestone that the market likely expected to de-risk the program instead left the central clinical question (which dose, if any, works) unresolved, while surfacing a meaningful progression rate in the evaluable population.

**Financial impact:** Material for a small-cap clinical-stage biotech — the share-price reaction (confirmed −62.7% screener / −63.6% ASX header per the 12:49pm sweep, traded value A$62.9m) implies the market is pricing a substantially reduced probability of near-term program success, which for a single-asset-dependent biotech threatens the clinical/commercial valuation case directly.

**Expectation/surprise:** Strong negative surprise — "modest efficacy" and an inconclusive dose-selection outcome is a materially worse result than a dose-finding success, and the scale of the share-price reaction confirms the market read it that way.

**Market/sector context:** Company-specific; broader ASX biotech sentiment not assessed as a driver here.

**Technical/price-reaction context:** Confirmed via TradingView/ASX-header cross-check in the 12:49pm sweep: −62.7% (screener) / −63.6% (ASX header), relative volume 97.3x, traded value A$62.9m — an extreme, high-conviction sell-off.

**Key risks:**
- Program-level strategic review is now pending; outcome could range from a redesigned trial to discontinuation.
- Early-stage biotech cash-runway risk if the program timeline extends.
- Dose Optimisation Committee recommended continued enrolment — a path still exists, but with no clear efficacy signal to date.

**What to watch:** Any company statement on the strategic review of the PTX-100 program; cash runway disclosure.
