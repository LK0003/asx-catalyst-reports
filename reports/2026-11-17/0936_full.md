**Time:** 8:30am–9:25am (Scheme Implementation Deed release + investor presentation) | **Catalyst type:** Takeover/Acquisition | **Price-sensitive:** Y | **Catalyst Score: 85/100** 🟢

**What changed:** Mayne Pharma has entered a Scheme Implementation Deed under which Gedeon Richter Plc (a 125-year-old Hungarian specialty pharma company) will acquire 100% of Mayne Pharma shares for **A$6.40 cash per share**, valuing equity at **~A$604 million**. The Board unanimously recommends the deal in the absence of a superior proposal and subject to an independent expert confirming it is in shareholders' best interests. Richter is funding the deal from internal cash resources — **no financing condition**. [Source: ASX announcement, MYX, 17 Nov 2026]

**Why it matters:** An unconditional-on-financing, Board-recommended cash takeover at a ~99% premium is about as unambiguous a positive catalyst as exists in this market — if it completes. This is Mayne's second attempted exit in roughly a year: a prior Scheme Implementation Deed with Cosette Pharmaceuticals (signed Feb 2025 at A$7.40/share) collapsed in Dec 2025 after Cosette tried to walk away and lost in court (Mayne was awarded ~$13.3m in costs). The new deal is priced lower (A$6.40 vs A$7.40) reflecting that history and the intervening sale of Mayne's International business to Evaris Pharma (announced 6 Nov 2026) — completion of that divestment is itself a **condition precedent** to this Scheme.

**Financial impact:** Equity value ~A$604m. Deal is binary (closes near A$6.40 or unwinds back toward a materially lower standalone price) — this is a "catalyst with event risk," not a graduated earnings move.

**Expectation/surprise:** No market consensus/rumour of a Richter bid was identified — this appears to be a genuine surprise announcement. Not previously flagged in any prior sweep this week.

**Key risks:**
- Multiple conditions precedent: FIRB, US Hart-Scott-Rodino antitrust clearance, a voluntary CFIUS declaration, Mayne shareholder vote, Court approval, no MAC, and **completion of the separate Evaris Pharma International Business Divestment** (itself subject to ACCC approval).
- Mayne's prior deal (Cosette) fell over in court — the market may price in some deal-completion discount to A$6.40 reflecting this history.
- Convertible noteholder Rubric Capital can elect to convert (at A$5.356/share, 7.69m shares) before the 31 Dec 2026 maturity, adding to the share count if the Scheme doesn't complete by then.

**What to watch:** Opening gap toward A$6.40 (minus a completion-risk discount); independent expert's report timing; CFIUS declaration outcome; ACCC progress on the separate Evaris divestment.

**Sources:** [ASX announcement — Scheme Implementation Deed, MYX, 17 Nov 2026]; prior-deal background from market research (Cosette termination, Dec 2025).

### 2. PDI — PDI Gold Limited (formerly Predictive Discovery)
**Time:** 9:26am | **Catalyst type:** Regulatory/Government-permit approval | **Price-sensitive:** Y | **Catalyst Score: 78/100** 🟢

**What changed:** The Government of Guinea has **granted Exploitation Permits** for PDI Gold's Bankan Project and the Mansounia deposit (part of the existing Kiniéro Gold Mine hub) — the final key regulatory approvals needed to commence construction at Bankan and mining at Mansounia. Permits issued under Presidential Decrees dated 16 Nov 2026, 15-year initial terms (Bankan 98.6km², Mansounia tenements 44.7km² and 26.5km²). The Guinean government holds a mandatory 15% free-carried interest per the Guinea Mining Code. [Source: ASX announcement, PDI, 17 Nov 2026]

**Why it matters:** Bankan is a Tier-1, largely undeveloped gold project — Probable Ore Reserve of 51.6Mt @ 1.78g/t Au for 2.95Moz, within a 4.14Moz Indicated + 1.23Moz Inferred resource. A June 2025 DFS outlined ~250,000oz/yr production over a 12-year mine life, US$463m upfront capex and US$1,111/oz AISC (at an assumed US$3,300/oz gold price — spot gold is currently well above that level, ~US$3,857/oz, materially improving projected project economics if that spread holds). Bankan + Kiniéro together target >400,000oz/yr once Bankan is in full production. Importantly, this removes a specific overhang: in May 2025 Guinea revoked over 100 exploration permits nationally (including some of PDI's Argo/Bokoro licences), which knocked PDI shares ~22% on fears about sovereign permitting risk generally. Today's grant is the clearest evidence yet that Bankan/Mansounia themselves were not caught up in that risk.

**Financial impact:** Unlocks a path to FID and construction on a project representing the large majority of PDI's resource base; company says an optimised execution schedule, capital cost estimate and production profile will be released "in early 2027."

**Expectation/surprise:** Permit had been in the approval pipeline for well over a year (application lodged Jan 2025); grant itself was anticipated but timing is a genuine positive surprise given the 2025 Guinea permitting scare.

**Key risks:** Guinea sovereign/political risk (permit revocations occurred elsewhere in-country within the last 18 months); US$463m capex still needs to be funded (financing/dilution risk ahead); compensation and resettlement activities still to be finalised; construction timeline/cost update not due until early 2027.

**What to watch:** Financing plan and updated capex/schedule (early 2027); any read-through to Kiniéro's existing near-term production/cost profile.

**Sources:** [ASX announcement — Bankan and Mansounia Exploitation Permits Granted, PDI, 17 Nov 2026]; background from prior Bankan DFS reporting (25 Jun 2025).
