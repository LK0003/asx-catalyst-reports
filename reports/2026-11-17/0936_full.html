Financial impact: Unlocks the path to FID/construction; updated capex/schedule due "early 2027."

Expectation/surprise: Anticipated eventually, but timing is a positive surprise given the 2025 permitting scare.

Key risks: Guinea sovereign/political risk; US$463m funding still required (dilution/financing risk); compensation/resettlement still to finalise.

What to watch: Financing plan; early-2027 updated schedule/capex; any further Guinean regulatory news.
