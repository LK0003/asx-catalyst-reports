## Lead item: NXT (NEXTDC) — M3 Melbourne power delay, FY27 guidance slashed

**FACT:** NXT released "M3 Melbourne Power Delay & Revised FY27 Guidance" at **8:35am** today (flagged price-sensitive on the ASX feed), resolving the trading-halt watch item carried over from this morning's pre-open sweep. Per secondary coverage (Fool.com.au, Proactive Investors, Bloomberg, Investing.com — **not yet cross-checked against the primary ASX PDF**):
- Jemena (the electricity distributor) has suspended parts of the network works needed to deliver the next increase in grid supply to M3, citing community/stakeholder opposition and lack of local council consent. Jemena's indicative timeline pushes the next supply increase to **FY28** (previously expected in FY27).
- Revised FY27 guidance excludes ~A$140m of net revenue and ~A$140m of underlying EBITDA tied to the affected M3 contracts:
  - Underlying EBITDA: **A$245–265m**, down from A$385–410m
  - Net revenue: **A$475–500m**, down from A$615–640m
  - Capex: **A$4,500–5,000m**, down from A$5,250–5,750m
- NXT says liquidity (~A$8.675bn at 30 Sept 2026) remains substantial and covenants are being met; it's reviewing capex, pausing uncontracted projects, and considering surplus site sales. Planning decisions on M3 Stages 6/7 await the Victorian state election (Nov 2026).
- Shares reportedly fell as much as ~42–44% on reopening to a near nine-year low (~A$5.26–5.32 vs ~A$9.18 prior close) — **price move not independently verified against a live quote**.

**INTERPRETATION:** This is a materially negative surprise, not priced in — it directly cuts forward EBITDA/revenue guidance by ~35-37% and pushes contracted capacity delivery out a full year due to a third-party (Jemena) grid/community-approval issue outside NXT's control. Passes the materiality test cleanly: it changes near-term earnings, isn't an MOU, and the ~40%+ reported share-price reaction is consistent with the scale of the downgrade. Primary ASX PDF not yet pulled — recommend verifying exact figures and the live price against asx.com.au/NXT directly.
