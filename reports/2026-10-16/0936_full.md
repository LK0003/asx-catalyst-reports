### 2. DYL — Deep Yellow — Tumas FID target withdrawn
**Time:** 8:26am AEDT ("Tumas Project (Namibia) update") · **Catalyst type:** Guidance down / Capital raising delay · **Price-sensitive:** Yes · **Catalyst Score: 61/100** · **Direction: 🔴**

**What changed:** Deep Yellow has withdrawn its target of taking a final investment decision on its flagship Tumas uranium project (Namibia) in Q4 2026, citing "continued weak equity market conditions" that constrain funding options for uranium developers. Construction is continuing regardless — civil works/earthworks are proceeding on schedule, and the company says it will keep advancing its funding strategy so Tumas is ready to proceed once market conditions improve. No new FID date was given.

**Why it matters:** This is DYL's third deferral of the Tumas FID (previously pushed from late 2024 to March 2025, then again in April 2025, and now again from the Q4 2026 target reaffirmed as recently as June 2026, when the company talked of a "clear run" to FID by year-end). A repeated pattern of deferrals despite management's own "clear run" language in June is a negative signal on execution/credibility, even though CEO Greg Field frames it as a funding-market issue rather than a project-quality one. The company states uranium market fundamentals remain strong — so this reads as an equity-financing-specific problem for DYL (and potentially uranium developers broadly) rather than a demand-side concern.

**What changed vs expectations:** A negative surprise — the company had explicitly reaffirmed a Q4 2026 FID target as recently as June 2026 ("clear run to FID by year-end"). Pushing it out again, with no new date, is a materially worse outcome than the guided timetable, even though the underlying asset (reserves, engineering ~79% complete, ~A$160m cash at June 2026) is unchanged.

**Financial impact:** No immediate balance-sheet impairment — construction continues and cash position (~A$160m as at 30 June 2026) remains intact — but the delay pushes out the start of project revenue/cash flow and keeps DYL in a longer pre-production, cash-burning phase, likely requiring further funding before FID.

**Market/sector context:** Read-through for other pre-FID uranium developers (e.g. AEE's Tiris BFS news today, by contrast, reads as the market-facing side of the story — see #3) given both cite the same macro environment; however, AEE's news today is framed positively (BFS "confirms" the economics) while DYL's is a deferral, so the sector signal is mixed rather than uniformly negative.

**Technical context:** Not verified — no live price data available.

**Key risks:**
- Repeated deferrals raise a credibility/execution-discipline question for management guidance generally.
- No new FID date disclosed — the catalyst (final investment decision + associated funding announcement) is now open-ended rather than dated, reducing near-term tradability of the thesis.
- Potential dilutive capital raise still likely needed ahead of any eventual FID.

**What to watch:** Opening price reaction in DYL; any capital raising or debt-financing announcement in coming weeks; comparison with peers' (AEE, BOE, PDN) financing progress to see if this is DYL-specific or sector-wide.
