What changed
CZR Resources, a WA iron-ore explorer, withdrew its off-market scrip takeover bid for Zuleika Gold (best-and-final 0.1742 CZR shares per ZAG share, declared 15 Oct; originally a ~$44.8m deal from June 2026). Why it matters
A clean, confirmable corporate-action failure. A scrip deal collapsing because the acquirer's own shareholders wouldn't back it is a governance signal about CZR's board as much as a loss of deal-premium for ZAG holders — it removes the ~$44.8m valuation reference ZAG had traded against since June.

Financial impact
Negative for ZAG (loses implied premium, reverts to standalone valuation). Mixed for CZR (avoids dilution, but failed vote signals weak shareholder confidence).

Expectation / surprise
The deal had already been re-priced "best and final" on 15 Oct, so resolution was expected soon — but withdrawal via a failed condition (rather than a vote win or extension) is a genuine negative surprise for ZAG holders.

Market / sector context
