Expectation/surprise: A clear negative surprise vs. the 2025 CMD framework — new, unanticipated information. The cost/job-cut numbers, framed as "efficiency," land alongside a cut to shareholder-return ambition, read negatively by the market.

Market/sector context: Brent's overnight -8.7% plunge pared back to just -0.56% (~US$94.09) by early afternoon — WDS's weakness is predominantly company-specific guidance disappointment, not a continuation of the overnight oil shock. Energy was the ASX 200's worst-performing sector (Viva Energy -5.16%, Santos -4.81%, Ampol -3.79%, Yancoal -3.62%), largely WDS-led.
