4. MEK — Meeka Metals — Ore Sorter Commissioning ("5x Grade Increase")
8:14am AEDT Production / Processing upgrade Price-sensitive: Y Score: 48/100 🟡 Direction
What changed
Commissioning data (26 Oct–15 Nov 2026) at the Murchison gold project's new Steinert ore sorter: feed averaged 0.86 g/t Au, product (to mill) 4.39 g/t Au — a ~5.1x uplift (rounded to "5x" in the headline). Mass pull to product was only 16% (15–19% range); gold recovery into that product stream 80–90%. Total feed to date: 5,412t over three weeks.
Why it matters
The ore sorter upgrade has been guided since March 2026; this is the first commissioning performance data.
Expectation / surprise — be skeptical
The headline "5x" is not the same metric as the company's own March 2026 guidance, which described sorting "85% of contained gold into 50% of rock mass" — a milder split implying roughly a 2x grade uplift on half the rock mass. The actual commissioning run used a much tighter cut (only ~16% of mass to product), which mechanically produces a larger grade multiple on a smaller, more selective fraction — at the cost of more material rejected to lower-grade stockpile. This is a three-week commissioning-stage product grade, not a steady-state average or final mill-recovered grade, and no blended run-of-mine uplift figure (the number that actually drives revenue) was disclosed. Treat "5x" as genuine but a narrower, more favourably-framed metric than the 2x guidance, not a clean apples-to-apples beat.
Key risks
Steady-state performance once both sorter lines run full-time is unconfirmed; primary ASX PDF not independently read.
What to watch at open
Whether the market reacts to the "5x" headline at face value or discounts it once the mass-pull detail is digested by analysts.
Sources: Discovery Alert — MEK ore sorter, The Market Online, Mar 2026, Business News
