Expectation/surprise: The existence of a downgrade is unsurprising given the stock's trajectory; the magnitude of the move (triggering a formal price query) is the surprise.

Market/sector context: Happening inside a risk-off session — ASX 200 down ~0.37% at 8,850 (2pm), after -1.79% Tuesday, driven partly by hot Australian wage data (+3.5% y/y vs 3.4% expected) reviving RBA rate-hike concern — a negative backdrop for leveraged property/construction names.

What to watch into the close: Whether the stock stabilises or extends losses; any follow-up from Lendlease, other brokers, or ASX Supervision.
