## New/material items since Friday evening

### 1. KMD (KMD Brands) — 7:35am, Price sensitive: Y
**"Update on non-binding indicative proposals received."** Per secondary coverage of the announcement: KMD has entered an **exclusivity arrangement with Bluestar Alliance LLC** (US consumer-brands group) to permit confirmatory due diligence and continued discussions on a possible transaction to acquire 100% of KMD shares, with a **long-stop date of 23 December 2026** (extendable on conditions). Company states no certainty of a binding proposal; shareholders advised no action required.
**Take:** Materially more concrete than the generic "indicative, non-binding, incomplete" language management used on the September earnings call — a named bidder plus exclusivity is a real escalation in a dual-listed (ASX/NZX) take-private process, though still non-binding with a ~7-week due-diligence runway and real execution risk (KMD rejected the Stokehouse/Rip Curl demerger proposal in March). Not verified beyond secondary-source summaries — original ASX/NZX PDF not independently pulled this scan. Source: [NZX announcement #482806](https://www.nzx.com/announcements/482806).
