## New/updated since 3:52pm cutoff

### INA (Ingenia Communities) — AVID's matching-right deadline has resolved: matched at $5.55 (up to $5.60), but board still hasn't recommended either bidder
- **What's new (fact):** The 3:52pm report flagged today (10 Nov) as AVID's deadline to table a further counterproposal against Warburg Pincus's $5.55 bid, with "no outcome confirmed as of ~3:41pm." Per Sharecafe (dated 10 Nov 2026; exact release time **not verified**, but not present in the ASX feed as checked at ~3:41pm), **AVID has exercised its matching right at $5.55 cash/stapled security** (up to $5.60 if a permitted H1-FY27 distribution of up to $0.05 is not deducted) — matching Warburg's total consideration.
- **Board decision:** Ingenia's board concluded, under the AVID Process Deed, that the matched proposal offers an "equal or superior outcome" to securityholders, so the AVID Process Deed (as amended) **remains active**. Critically, the board has **not** determined the proposal is a "Bidder Superior Proposal" under the separate Peet scheme implementation deed, and has **not** indicated it will recommend AVID's proposal. Ingenia says it continues to comply with the Peet SID and is still working toward the Peet acquisition.
- **New dates:** Exclusivity end date **extended to 14 December 2026**; Warburg Pincus/Discovery Parks/LDR Capital retain due-diligence access until **13 November 2026** under a standard 4-day post-match exception.
- **Materiality take:** This is a genuine partial resolution of today's single most time-sensitive item, but it is **not a deal outcome** — the three-way contest continues, now with a ~5-week runway before the next exclusivity deadline, and Warburg retains a short DD window to potentially respond again. Price-sensitive: **Yes**. Treat dollar figures as indicative pending the primary ASX PDF.
