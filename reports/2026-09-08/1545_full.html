Why it matters

Business conditions fell 5 points to -1, the lowest since August 2020; confidence fell 2 points to -8 (12 points below January). Profitability fell 10 points to -9, a new post-COVID low, as purchase-cost growth (+2.3% q/q) continued to outpace product-price growth (+0.8% q/q). Conditions fell in six of eight industries, led by construction, mining and manufacturing; Victoria and WA saw the largest declines (-13 points each). This is a genuine, data-confirmed weakening of the domestic economy — not merely rate-hike speculation — landing the same day as a sharp consumer-sentiment slide and a fresh housing-downturn data point.
