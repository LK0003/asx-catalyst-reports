An additional ~1,700m of drilling is scheduled to complete the season.

- **Price-sensitive:** Y

- **Direction:** 🟢
- **Why it matters:** Near-10% copper over meaningful widths (24.8m) is an exceptional grade by porphyry/skarn-style deposit standards, and the reported downhole extension of mineralisation ~170m below the current resource envelope is the kind of result that can support a future resource upgrade — which the company itself has flagged via the plan to re-assay historical pulps.
- **What changed:** New, materially higher-grade assays than the existing JORC resource average; a genuine sense that the deposit may be larger/deeper than currently modelled.
- **Financial impact:** PXX is a micro-cap (share price ~A$0.019); the current JORC Mineral Resource Estimate is 7.2Mt @ 3.1% Cu, 6.5g/t Ag (224,375t contained Cu). These are drill-result-stage numbers only — no resource upgrade or economic study has been released confirming a change to the contained-metal figure.
- **Expectation/surprise:** Not verified against any formal consensus (PXX has no broker coverage identified) — but the grades reported are subjectively surprising relative to typical exploration results.
- **Market/sector context:** Broadly supportive — global copper demand narrative (electrification, grid, AI/data-centre power) remains a structural tailwind for copper explorers.
- **Technical context:** Not verified.
- **Key risks:**
  - Drill-result-stage only — not yet a resource upgrade or feasibility study; translating high-grade intercepts into a materially larger economic resource is not guaranteed.
  - Micro-cap, thin liquidity — price moves can be volatile and difficult to trade in size.
  - Historical pulp re-assay program is exploratory in nature and its outcome is unknown.
- **What to watch at/after open:** Volume and price reaction at the open as a read on how much of this was already absorbed intraday on 9 Sept versus still being digested this morning.

- **What the market may be reacting to:** The prospect of a future resource upgrade at Caribou Dome, and the read-through from re-assaying historical pulps.
