Section 8 — Best 3 to Research Further

1. ASB — Austal

Why it made the list: The only genuinely new, confirmed, price-reactive catalyst of the entire day — a real competing-bidder dynamic for a major asset sale.

What the market may be reacting to: The implied upward repricing of Austal USA's sale value and the credibility competitive tension lends to the sale process.

What would confirm/invalidate: Confirmed by Hanwha countering, or Wildcat's diligence concluding with a firm offer near the indicated range; invalidated by a Wildcat withdrawal, a regulatory block, or an eventual sale price undershooting both ranges.

Biggest risk: Both proposals are non-binding and conditional; US naval-shipbuilding assets typically draw elevated government/regulatory scrutiny of any change of control.

What to monitor: Hanwha's response, Wildcat's financing disclosure, US regulatory commentary, Austal's board recommendation timeline.

2. GML — Gateway Mining

Why it made the list: Resolves the largest pending halt with the most information-dense outcome — a large, credibly-backed raise funding a clearly-scoped drilling program.

What the market may be reacting to: The trade-off between meaningful dilution and a well-funded exploration runway.

What would confirm/invalidate: A reopen price holding up despite dilution would confirm confidence in the program; a reopen sell-off would suggest the market prices this as pure dilution.

Biggest risk: This is a funding event, not a resource/production catalyst — the market may wait for actual drilling results.

What to monitor: Reopen price/volume; drilling timeline and first assays.

3. PAT — Patriot Resources

Why it made the list: A rare instance today of resolved ambiguity landing on the positive side (de-risking) rather than the more common dilutive-raise outcome.

What the market may be reacting to: Removal of a permitting overhang at a project with an existing 31.4Moz AgEq inferred resource.

What would confirm/invalidate: A positive reopen reaction and timely drill-program start would confirm; a delay despite the agreement would suggest the market over-read the news.

Biggest risk: An access agreement is necessary but not sufficient for value creation; PAT is thinly traded and reactions can be noisy.

What to monitor: Confirmed reopen reaction; drill program start date and interim assays.
