What would confirm/invalidate: A prompt, credible explanation and a swift, well-regarded permanent-MD appointment would de-risk this; continued silence or further departures would confirm elevated risk.

Biggest risk: This could equally resolve as a routine, low-risk internal promotion — the negative framing here is a hypothesis based on "termination" language, not a confirmed dispute.

Monitor: Company statements, board commentary, BC8 price/volume reaction at and after open.
