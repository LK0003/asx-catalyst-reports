2. MYX — Mayne Pharma GroupScore 47/100
🟢 Direction (narrow win, not a deal revival)1:29pm (halted since 8:20am)Type: LitigationPrice-sensitive: Yes

Announcement: "Supreme Court Decision in Cosette Proceedings."

Why it matters

The NSW Court of Appeal has dismissed Cosette Pharmaceuticals' appeal against the October 2025 Supreme Court ruling, affirming that Cosette had no right to terminate its scheme implementation deed with Mayne and rejecting Cosette's material-adverse-change and misleading-conduct claims, with costs awarded to Mayne. This is not a revival of the ~$672m Cosette acquisition of Mayne — that deal is dead regardless, killed when Treasurer Jim Chalmers refused FIRB approval over concerns about closure of Mayne's Salisbury, SA manufacturing site. It removes Cosette's last avenue to unwind costs orders already paid (~$14.4m received by Mayne) and strengthens Mayne's position for a separate, unquantified damages claim against Cosette and its controllers.

What changed

Confirmed court outcome today, after an 8:20am trading halt pending the ruling.

Financial impact

Modest and mostly defensive — locks in costs already recovered, adds a fresh costs order, improves negotiating position for the damages claim (quantum unknown).

Expectation/surprise

Low-to-moderate — Mayne had already won at first instance and on costs twice, so an appeal loss for Cosette fits the pattern. Cosette has until 11 November 2026 to seek High Court special leave — not yet confirmed whether it will.

Market/sector context

Pharma-specific, not sector-wide.

Technical/price reaction

Not verified — no intraday data accessible.

Key risks

  • Possible High Court special-leave application by Cosette (window to 11 Nov 2026) would prolong the overhang.
  • Damages claim against Cosette's controllers is unquantified and unresolved — today is a procedural win, not a cash settlement.

What to watch

Any special-leave filing by Cosette; progress/quantum on Mayne's damages claim.
