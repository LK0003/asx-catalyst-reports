**Fact:** RRL was in a trading halt from 9:30am this morning (reason not disclosed at halt time). At 11:50am it announced the Federal Court (Justice Stellios) has ruled the Commonwealth's Section 10 Aboriginal heritage declaration over part of the ~$1.1bn McPhillamys gold project site (near Blayney, NSW) is invalid, and remitted the matter to the current Environment Minister (Murray Watt) for redetermination. Trading was set to resume following the announcement. Per secondary sources, the project's ore reserve is 56Mt at 1.1g/t for 1.89Moz gold, and RRL is separately pursuing an alternative waste-landform design that would bypass the affected heritage area regardless of the redetermination outcome.

**Interpretation (not verified as market reaction — no live/intraday price data accessible):** This is genuinely material — it overturns the legal basis of the 2024 ministerial block that had stalled McPhillamys, a project in the ~$1bn+ range for a company RRL's size. However, it is not an unconditional green light: the declaration being "invalid" sends the heritage question back to the minister for redetermination, not a guarantee the project proceeds unencumbered — a fresh declaration is still possible. The existence of RRL's parallel IWL (bypass) design suggests management itself isn't betting entirely on a favourable redetermination. Treat as a clearly positive legal/regulatory catalyst that de-risks the path to development, but not as confirmed project approval.
