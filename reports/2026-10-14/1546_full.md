### 2. MYX — Mayne Pharma Group
**Time:** 1:29pm (halted since 8:20am) | **Type:** Litigation | **Price-sensitive:** Yes | **Direction:** 🟢 (narrow legal win; not a deal revival) | **Catalyst Score: 47/100**

**Announcement:** "Supreme Court Decision in Cosette Proceedings."

**Why it matters:** The NSW Court of Appeal has dismissed Cosette Pharmaceuticals' appeal against the October 2025 Supreme Court ruling, affirming in full that Cosette had no right to terminate its scheme implementation deed with Mayne and rejecting Cosette's material-adverse-change and misleading-conduct claims. The court also ordered Cosette to pay Mayne's appeal costs. This is **not** a revival of the ~$672m Cosette acquisition of Mayne — that deal is dead regardless, having been separately killed when Treasurer Jim Chalmers refused FIRB approval over concerns Cosette would close Mayne's Salisbury, SA manufacturing site. What this does do is remove Cosette's last live avenue to unwind the earlier costs orders (Mayne has already received ~$14.4m from Cosette for legal costs) and strengthens Mayne's position ahead of a separate damages claim it is pursuing against Cosette and its controllers.

**What changed:** Confirmed court outcome, today, after an 8:20am trading halt pending the ruling.

**Financial impact:** Modest and mostly defensive — locks in costs already recovered, adds a fresh costs order in Mayne's favour, and improves its negotiating position for the damages claim (quantum not yet known). Does not reopen the original $672m sale.

**Expectation/surprise:** Low-to-moderate — Mayne had already won at first instance and on costs twice; an appeal loss for Cosette is the more probable outcome on the pattern, so this confirms rather than surprises. Cosette has until 11 November 2026 to seek special leave to appeal to the High Court — not yet confirmed whether it will.

**Market/sector context:** Pharma-specific, not sector-wide.

**Technical/price reaction:** Not verified — no intraday data accessible.

**Key risks:**
- Possible High Court special leave application by Cosette (window open to 11 Nov 2026) would prolong the overhang.
- The damages claim against Cosette's controllers is unquantified and unresolved — today's ruling is a procedural win, not a cash settlement.

**What to watch:** Any special-leave filing by Cosette; progress/quantum on Mayne's separate damages claim.

**Sources:** [Kalkine — Mayne Pharma Court of Appeal win](https://kalkinemedia.com/au/news/announcements/mayne-pharma-secures-victory-as-nsw-court-of-appeal-rejects-cosettes-appeal-and-grants-costs); [Capital Brief — Cosette loses appeal bid](https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/cosette-loses-bid-to-overturn-failed-material-adverse-change-claim-against-mayne-pharma-73b20e9e-651f-4bbf-8fff-3783d008909b/); [ASX MYX announcements feed](https://www.asx.com.au/asx/v2/statistics/announcements.do?by=asxCode&asxCode=MYX)
