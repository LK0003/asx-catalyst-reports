### 6. KRM — Kingsrose Mining — Score 49 🟢
**Time:** Resolved this afternoon (originally flagged ~10:16am) | **Type:** Government-permit/Regulatory approval | **Price-sensitive:** Yes

**What changed:** Finland's Tukes mining authority has **GRANTED** the Penikat Area 6 exploration permit (not a rejection, resolving this morning's ambiguity) — allowing 5,000m/year of drilling (1 Aug–31 Mar window), valid 4 years once legally binding, extendable to a maximum 15 years. Tukes also issued a separate Enforcement Order permitting limited drilling (3,000m/year) even if the permit itself is appealed, with buffer zones (100m wooded/200m cleared) from Natura 2000 areas. **Financial impact:** Enables the planned Jan 2027 drilling campaign to proceed; no direct dollar figure.

**Expectation/surprise:** Moderate — a permit grant was the expected/hoped-for outcome, but given the adverse Area 1 precedent, confirmation removes genuine downside-case risk.

**Market/sector context:** No direct macro driver; jurisdiction-specific regulatory risk reduction.

**Key risks:**
- 20 Nov 2026 appeal window remains open — the permit is not yet fully unappealable.
- The Enforcement Order mechanism (allowing drilling despite an appeal) could itself become contested.

**What to watch:** Whether any appeal is lodged before 20 Nov; drilling mobilisation ahead of the Jan 2027 target.
