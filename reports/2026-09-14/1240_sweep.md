- **Time:** Reported today, 14 Sept 2026 (exact ASX timestamp not verified)
- **Price-sensitive:** Y

Not yet a "done deal" catalyst; would become materially more significant on an actual signed implementation deed.

- **Source:** [NRW Holdings secures $313M contract extensions (Grafa)](https://grafa.com/en/news/australia/nrw-holdings-secures-313m-contract-extensions)

### 3. CYL — Catalyst Metals — Trident underground reserve upgrade (+32%)
- **Time:** Reported today, 14 Sept 2026, appears to have landed shortly after the 10:16 cutoff (search snippet timestamped "~2 hours ago" as of this scan)
- **Price-sensitive:** Y
- **What's new:** Underground probable ore reserve at the Trident deposit (Plutonic gold belt, WA) increased 32% to 524,000oz (3.9Mt at 4.2g/t), up from the 397,000oz reserve disclosed in April 2026 — though average grade fell from 5.1g/t to 4.2g/t. Trident is guided to produce 60,000–80,000ozpa at steady state over a ~10-year mine life; first ore still not expected until 2027.
- **Materiality take:** A reserve upgrade at an already-known, already-guided development asset — extends mine life/production visibility rather than introducing a new resource or bringing forward cash flow. Reported as the ASX 200 session's leaderboard-topper this morning, so the market is already pricing a positive reaction; the lower average grade is a partial offset worth noting rather than an unambiguous positive.
- **Source:** [Catalyst lifts Trident gold reserves 32 per cent (Australian Mining)](https://www.australianmining.com.au/catalyst-lifts-trident-gold-reserves-32-per-cent)
